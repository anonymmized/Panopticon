## Panopticon 1.0.0

- Native panel with persistent pins, spaces, folders, collections and configurable widgets.
- English and Russian interface, native menus and independent window/panel appearance.
- Native-style hover springs, launch feedback, reduced motion and deterministic auto-hide.
- Offline profile pins, cancellable folders and bounded, persistent focus sessions.
- Accessible toggle labels, localized numbers and refreshed status messages.
- Bundle-resource validation, verified backups and installer rollback preservation.
- Illustrated bilingual documentation and a reproducible local acceptance command.

This is a local release candidate. Public signing, user-driven integration checks and
publication remain separate. See [release readiness](docs/RELEASE.md).
